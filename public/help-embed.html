OFFICIAL CAMPUS PROCEDURE

Central Security Lost & Found Office

All items unclaimed on the portal for more than 48 hours are automatically transferred to the Central Security Locker in Room 102.

Standard Operating Procedure

  1. Bring your official College Student or Staff ID card.
  2. Provide the unique CampusFind Item Reference ID from the portal.
  3. For electronic devices, demonstrate unlocking the screen pattern or entering your passcode.
  4. Sign the physical physical handover ledger before departing.
Office Hours: Mon – Fri: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Location: Student Services Center, Ground Floor, Room 102
